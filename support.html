Common Questions (FAQ)

How do I open the add-on in Google Meet?

Click the Activities icon (triangle, square, circle) in the bottom-right corner of your Meet call and select "Telehealth Add-on."

Can patients see my private notes?

No. Patients only see what you choose to "Push to Main Stage." Your side panel remains strictly private to the provider.

The app isn't loading, what should I do?

Ensure you are logged into the same Google account that has the add-on installed and refresh your Meet browser tab.
